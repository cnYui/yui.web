AI Native Developer

For me, AI is not a plugin layered on top of a product. It is often one of the product's core runtime components, and the product loses meaning without it.

From Coding to Reviewing

The developer's value is shifting from writing every line by hand to defining requirements, feeding context, reviewing outputs, integrating systems, and validating results.

Vibe -> Spec -> Harness

My preferred loop is: explore the idea, write the spec, set constraints, let agents implement, and keep testing until the product is convincing enough to ship.

Product Judgment Wins

In hackathons and real products alike, the strongest advantage is not shipping more features. It is making better product judgments, building a tighter demo, and closing the loop faster.
